Screenator Hints

Introduction

  • Screenator has two annotation modes: Screen and Paper.
  • Tools can be selected from the Floating Icon, Tool Bar, and System Tray Icon.

Global Shortcuts

  • F3 switches between Screen and Paper annotation mode.
  • F9 shows or hides the floating icon.
  • F10 shows or hides the toolbar.
  • Tool selection: F8 Pen, F7 Text, F6 Arrow, F12 Timer.
  • F4 saves a screenshot in the Pictures folder.
  • Esc closes shape handles and exits the current tool in Screen mode.

Paper Mode Shortcuts

  • Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+Z redo.
  • Ctrl+T toggles visibility.
  • Ctrl+Q exits Paper mode.
  • Alt+F4 closes Screenator.
  • Ctrl+W shows the paper as a window.
  • Ctrl+D shows the desktop panel.

Mouse in Screen Mode

  • Left Button draws using the active tool.
  • Middle Buttonk activates the eraser.
  • Short Right click closes shape handles and exits the current tool.
  • Long Right Click opens the Scroll Mode menu and chooses Color or Thickness.
  • Mouse wheel changes color or thickness.

Mouse in Paper Mode

  • Left click draws using the active tool.
  • Middle click activates the eraser.
  • Right click closes shape handles, then acts as eraser without exiting the current tool.

Stylus

  • In your stylus configuration, set Button 1 (the one nearest the tip) to Middle Click, and Button 2 to Right Click.
  • A short press of Button 1 acts as the mouse wheel in Screen mode and as a middle click in Paper mode.
  • A long press of Button 1 acts as a middle click in Screen mode.
  • A short press of Button 2 acts as a right click; a long press in Screen mode opens the Scroll Mode menu.
  • Button 1 also changes the active text or shape color.

Text Tool

  • After typing, press Enter, right-click, or stylus Button 2 to accept the text and re-enable the mouse. Esc cancels the text.

Marker Tool

  • Long right click or Long press Button 2 opens the Marker Opacity menu.

Saving

  • Screenshots are saved automatically in the Pictures folder with the current date-time stamp.
  • Paper PDFs are saved automatically in the Documents folder with the current date-time stamp.
